Himalayan Lake & Trekking Destination

Deoria Tal

Discover the peaceful beauty of Deoria Tal, a scenic Himalayan lake famous for its crystal-clear water, surrounding forests and stunning reflections of the Chaukhamba mountain range.

Deoria Tal is a beautiful high-altitude lake located near Sari village in Rudraprayag district, Uttarakhand. Surrounded by dense forests and Himalayan mountains, it is especially famous for the reflection of the Chaukhamba peaks in its calm water on clear mornings. The short uphill trek from Sari makes it a popular destination for nature lovers and beginners.

Famous Views & Places

  • Deoria Tal Lake
  • Chaukhamba mountain reflection
  • Surrounding oak and rhododendron forests
  • Himalayan sunrise viewpoints
  • Nearby Sari village

Things to Do

  • Trek from Sari village to the lake.
  • Enjoy the reflection of Himalayan peaks.
  • Explore the forest walking trails.
  • Enjoy bird watching and nature photography.
  • Combine the visit with Chopta and Tungnath.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November are generally good periods to visit. October and November often offer clear skies and excellent opportunities to see the Chaukhamba reflection.

Monsoon rain can make the trail slippery and reduce mountain visibility. Winter visits may involve snow and very cold conditions.

Culture & Local Significance

Deoria Tal is connected with local Garhwali traditions and religious beliefs. Local legends associate the lake with the Pandavas and the Yaksha Prashna story from the Mahabharata.

History & Local Significance

Deoria Tal is considered sacred by many local people. According to a popular traditional story, the lake is associated with the episode in which a Yaksha questioned the Pandavas. It is also connected with local beliefs about the gods and the natural world.

These stories are part of regional religious tradition and folklore; they should not be treated as independently verified historical facts.

Deoria Tal Trek Details

  • Location: Near Sari village, Rudraprayag district
  • Altitude: Approximately 2,438 metres
  • Starting point: Sari village
  • Trek distance: Around 2.5–3 km one way
  • Trek duration: Around 1–2 hours uphill for many visitors
  • Difficulty: Easy to moderate
  • Experience: Lake views, forest trails and Himalayan scenery

Walking time depends on fitness, weather and trail conditions. The uphill route includes a steady climb.

Popular Trek Route

Sari Village to Deoria Tal

The popular route begins at Sari village. From there, visitors follow an uphill trail through forested slopes and stone pathways to reach the lake. The walk is short but includes a steady climb.

Deoria Tal to Chopta Route

Experienced trekkers sometimes continue from Deoria Tal towards Rohini Bugyal and Chopta. This is a longer mountain route and should be attempted with proper planning and local guidance.

Follow forest department instructions and local guidance. Check the current trekking route and access conditions before starting.

Nearby Attractions

  • Chopta: A scenic Himalayan destination known for its meadows, forests and mountain views.
  • Tungnath Temple: A famous high-altitude Shiva temple reached by a trek from Chopta.
  • Chandrashila: A popular summit offering wide Himalayan views.
  • Ukhimath: A religious town known for its temples and cultural significance.
  • Sari Village: The main starting point for the Deoria Tal trek.

How to Reach Deoria Tal

By Air

Jolly Grant Airport near Dehradun is the nearest major airport. From there, travellers can continue by road towards Rudraprayag, Ukhimath and Sari village.

By Train

Rishikesh and Haridwar are commonly used railway connections for reaching this region. From either station, travellers can continue by road.

By Road

Reach Ukhimath by road via Rudraprayag. From Ukhimath, continue towards Sari village. The remaining journey to Deoria Tal is completed on foot via the uphill trekking trail.

Camping & Accommodation

Sari village offers accommodation options such as homestays and guesthouses. Visitors can stay there before or after the trek. Camping and overnight stays near the lake are subject to current forest department rules and designated camping arrangements.

Do not assume lakeside camping is permitted. Confirm current restrictions and approved camping locations with the local forest authorities.

Visitor Tips

  • Wear comfortable shoes with a good grip.
  • Carry warm clothes, as temperatures can drop quickly.
  • Start early to enjoy the lake in calm morning conditions.
  • Carry drinking water and basic first-aid supplies.
  • Download offline maps before starting the trek.
  • Mobile network coverage may be limited near the lake.
  • Follow forest entry rules and local instructions.
  • Do not leave plastic or other waste behind.

Responsible Travel

Deoria Tal is an important natural and scenic destination. Keep the lake and surrounding forest clean, avoid disturbing birds and wildlife, respect local traditions and support responsible local tourism services.
