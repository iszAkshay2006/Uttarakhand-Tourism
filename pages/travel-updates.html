Your Guide to
Uttarakhand

Discover useful travel information to explore Uttarakhand thoughtfully. Plan your journey through its mountains, sacred towns, scenic valleys, and peaceful nature escapes.

🧭

Plan Your Journey

Choose your destinations, check travel routes, and allow enough time for mountain roads and local sightseeing.

🎒

Pack Smart

Carry comfortable walking shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, essential medicines, and reusable water bottles.

🌿

Travel Responsibly

Keep natural areas clean, respect local customs, avoid single-use plastics, and support local businesses.

⛰️

Mountain Safety

Check weather and road conditions before travelling. Follow local advisories and use authorised guides for demanding treks.

Before You Travel

Travel conditions, opening hours, permits, and road accessibility may change. Verify current information with official sources before starting your journey.
