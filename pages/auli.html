Auli is a beautiful hill destination in Chamoli district of Uttarakhand. Surrounded by snow-covered Himalayan mountains, it is famous for skiing, scenic landscapes and peaceful natural beauty.

1. About Auli

Auli is one of the most popular mountain destinations in Uttarakhand. It attracts visitors with its beautiful valleys, snow-covered slopes and breathtaking Himalayan views.

2. History of Auli

Auli is known for its connection with the Himalayan region and traditional mountain life. Over time, it has developed into a popular destination for winter sports and tourism.

3. Geography and Location

Auli is located in Chamoli district of Uttarakhand in the Garhwal Himalayas. Its high-altitude location offers beautiful views of surrounding Himalayan peaks.

4. Things to Do in Auli

Visitors can enjoy skiing during winter, take the ropeway ride, explore mountain trails and admire the beautiful Himalayan scenery.

5. Best Time to Visit

December to March is popular for snow and skiing. During warmer months, visitors can enjoy pleasant weather and mountain views.

6. How to Reach Auli

The nearest major railway station is Rishikesh. Visitors can travel by road towards Joshimath and continue to Auli.
