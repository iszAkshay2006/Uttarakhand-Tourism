Kumaon Region • Uttarakhand, India

Ranikhet

Almora district, Uttarakhand

Ranikhet is a beautiful hill station in the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand. Known for its peaceful surroundings, pine forests and stunning Himalayan views, it is a wonderful destination for nature lovers.

1. About Ranikhet

Ranikhet is a charming hill station located in Almora district. Its name means "Queen's Meadow". The town is famous for its pleasant weather, green landscapes and beautiful mountain views.

2. Famous Attractions

Popular attractions include Chaubatia Garden, Jhula Devi Temple, Haidakhan Babaji Temple, Bhalu Dam and the Kumaon Regimental Centre Museum.

3. Natural Beauty

Ranikhet is surrounded by pine, oak and deodar forests. Visitors can enjoy beautiful views of the Himalayan peaks, green valleys and peaceful mountain scenery.

4. Things to Do

Visitors can enjoy nature walks, photography, sightseeing, temple visits, forest trails and relaxing in the peaceful surroundings of the hill station.

5. Best Time to Visit

March to June is generally pleasant for sightseeing. September to November is also a good period to enjoy clear mountain views and the natural beauty of Ranikhet.

6. How to Reach

Ranikhet is connected by road with nearby towns and cities. Kathgodam is a major nearby railway station, while Pantnagar Airport is the nearest airport.

7. Local Food and Culture

Visitors can enjoy traditional Kumaoni dishes such as Bhatt ki Churkani, Aloo ke Gutke and local sweets like Bal Mithai. The town also reflects the rich culture and traditions of the Kumaon region.
