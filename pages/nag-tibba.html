Popular Himalayan Trek

Nag Tibba

Experience the beauty of Nag Tibba, a popular Himalayan trek known for its forest trails, peaceful campsites, mountain views and exciting trekking adventure.

Nag Tibba, also known as the Serpent's Peak, is located in the Garhwal region of Uttarakhand. It is a popular weekend trekking destination for beginners and adventure lovers. The trek offers beautiful forest trails, open meadows, camping experiences and panoramic Himalayan views.

Famous Places & Views

  • Nag Tibba Summit
  • Nag Devta Temple
  • Nag Tibba Base Camp
  • Khatian campsite
  • Panoramic Himalayan viewpoints

Things to Do

  • Enjoy trekking through forest trails.
  • Experience overnight camping.
  • Watch beautiful sunrise and sunset views.
  • Capture Himalayan landscapes.
  • Explore local villages and nature.

Best Time to Visit

October to June is generally suitable for trekking. April to June offers pleasant weather, while December to March may bring snowfall on the upper trail and summit.

Monsoon conditions can make trails slippery and increase travel risks. Always check local weather and trail conditions before starting.

Culture & Local Significance

The name Nag Tibba is associated with Nag Devta, the serpent deity worshipped by local communities. Villagers traditionally visit the temple to offer prayers for the protection of their livestock.

History & Local Significance

Nag Tibba holds religious importance for nearby villages. The name combines "Nag", associated with Nag Devta, and "Tibba", a local word for a hill or peak. A temple dedicated to Nag Devta is located near the summit and is visited by local people.

Nag Tibba Trek Details

  • Region: Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand
  • Summit altitude: Approximately 3,022 metres
  • Popular starting point: Pantwari village
  • Typical duration: 2 days
  • Difficulty: Easy to moderate
  • Experience: Trekking, camping and Himalayan views

Distance, duration and difficulty depend on the chosen route, weather and trekking group's pace.

Popular Trek Route

Day 1: Pantwari to Khatian Campsite

The popular route starts from Pantwari village. Trekkers pass through village paths, forest trails and the locally revered Nag Devta temple before reaching the Khatian campsite. The first day's walk usually takes around 5–6 hours.

Day 2: Campsite to Nag Tibba Summit

Start early and follow the forest trail towards Nag Tibba Base Camp. From there, continue uphill to the summit, also known as Nag Tibba Jhandi. On clear days, trekkers can enjoy views of surrounding Himalayan peaks.

Trek timings are approximate. Follow your guide's instructions and avoid continuing in unsafe weather.

Nearby Attractions

  • Mussoorie: A popular hill station known for mountain views and scenic attractions.
  • Thatyur: A small settlement in the Tehri Garhwal region that serves as an approach point for another Nag Tibba trekking route.
  • Dewalsari: A forest destination known for its natural surroundings and trekking trails.
  • Panthwari: A village commonly used as the starting point for the Nag Tibba trek.

How to Reach Nag Tibba

By Air

Jolly Grant Airport near Dehradun is the nearest major airport. From there, travellers can continue by road towards Pantwari or other trekking starting points.

By Train

Dehradun Railway Station is a convenient railway connection for travellers. From Dehradun, taxis and other road transport can be used to reach the trekking base village.

By Road

Pantwari is commonly reached from Dehradun via Mussoorie and Nainbagh. The drive takes several hours depending on traffic and road conditions.

Visitor Tips

  • Wear comfortable trekking shoes with good grip.
  • Carry warm clothes, especially during winter.
  • Take sufficient drinking water because water sources can be limited.
  • Carry a rain jacket and basic first-aid supplies.
  • Keep a torch, power bank and personal medicines.
  • Mobile network coverage may be limited on the trail.
  • Consider an experienced local guide, especially in winter.
  • Carry all waste back and keep the trekking route clean.

Responsible Travel

Nag Tibba is a beautiful natural destination. Help protect its forests and mountain environment by avoiding plastic litter, respecting local religious places, supporting local guides and homestays, and not disturbing wildlife.
