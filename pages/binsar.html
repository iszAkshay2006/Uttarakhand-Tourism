About Binsar

Binsar is a beautiful hill destination in the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand, situated at an altitude of around 2,420 metres. It was once the summer capital of the Chand Dynasty rulers. Binsar is famous for its peaceful surroundings, dense oak and rhododendron forests, wildlife and spectacular views of Himalayan peaks such as Nanda Devi, Trishul and Panchachuli.

Binsar is an ideal destination for nature lovers, photographers, bird watchers and travellers looking for a peaceful mountain getaway.

Popular Attractions

Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary

A beautiful forest sanctuary known for oak, rhododendron and pine trees. It is home to animals such as barking deer, wild boar, Himalayan black bears and many species of birds.

Zero Point

A popular viewpoint inside the sanctuary. A short trek through the forest leads to panoramic views of the Himalayan mountain ranges.

Kasar Devi Temple

An ancient temple near Almora, known for its peaceful atmosphere, spiritual importance and beautiful views of the surrounding hills.

Bineshwar Mahadev Temple

A historic temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. It is associated with the name and history of the Binsar region.

Gananath Temple

Known for its natural caves and ancient Shiva temple, Gananath is a peaceful religious destination near Almora.

Khali Estate

A scenic heritage property surrounded by forests, offering beautiful views of the Himalayan mountains.

Things to Do in Binsar

Nature Walks

Enjoy peaceful walks through oak and rhododendron forests while listening to the sounds of birds.

Wildlife Safari

Explore Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary through a jeep safari and experience its rich natural surroundings.

Trekking

Take a trek to Zero Point or explore nearby forest trails for beautiful mountain and valley views.

Bird Watching

Binsar is a wonderful destination for bird lovers, with many resident and migratory bird species found in its forests.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and October to December are generally considered good months to visit Binsar. During these periods, visitors can enjoy pleasant weather, forest walks and clear mountain views.

How to Reach Binsar

By Air

The nearest airport is Pantnagar Airport. Taxis are available from the airport to reach the Almora and Binsar region.

By Train

Kathgodam is the nearest major railway station. From there, visitors can hire a taxi or use road transport towards Almora and Binsar.

By Road

Binsar is connected by road through Almora. Taxis and buses are available from nearby towns and cities.

Nearby Town

Almora is the nearest major town and an important base for travelling to Binsar and nearby attractions.
