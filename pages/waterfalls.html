🌱 Enjoy Waterfalls Responsibly

  • Check weather forecasts and local advisories before visiting.
  • Avoid entering fast-flowing water, especially during heavy rain.
  • Wear footwear with a good grip on wet and slippery trails.
  • Follow safety signs and stay away from restricted areas.
  • Keep the surroundings clean and carry your waste back.
