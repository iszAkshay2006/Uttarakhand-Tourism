Kedarnath Temple

Kedarnath, Rudraprayag District, Uttarakhand

Home About History Geography Worship

Kedarnath Temple is one of the most sacred Hindu shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva. Located high in the Himalayas, it is traditionally associated with Panch Kedar, the twelve Jyotirlingas and the Uttarakhand Char Dham pilgrimage.

1. Which Deity Is Worshipped Here?

Lord Shiva is worshipped at Kedarnath in the form of a sacred, naturally shaped stone lingam. According to traditional belief, this form represents the hump of the divine bull associated with Shiva.

2. Who Visits and What Do They Pray For?

Pilgrims from across India and around the world visit to offer prayers to Lord Shiva. Devotees commonly pray for peace, spiritual strength, protection, forgiveness and the well-being of their families. The shrine is an important destination on the Himalayan pilgrimage circuit.

3. Religious Beliefs, Vows and Legends

Many devotees make religious vows, perform puja and seek blessings during difficult periods of life. A traditional legend tells how the Pandavas sought Lord Shiva after the Mahabharata war. This account belongs to religious tradition rather than independently verified history.

4. History and Sacred Traditions

Kedarnath is one of the most revered temples in the Himalayas. Hindu traditions associate the shrine with the Pandavas and Adi Shankaracharya. In 2013, devastating floods affected the surrounding region, while the main temple structure survived.

6. Geography and Access

Kedarnath lies at approximately 3,584 metres above sea level near the Mandakini River, surrounded by spectacular Himalayan peaks. The traditional trek from Gaurikund is roughly 16–19 km, depending on route arrangements. Weather and trail conditions can change, so check current local guidance before travelling.

7. Worship Practices and Visitor Advice

Visitors commonly attend darshan, aarti and Shiva puja and make offerings according to temple rules. The shrine operates seasonally because of heavy Himalayan snowfall. Confirm current opening dates, registration requirements and travel advisories through official sources before planning a visit.
