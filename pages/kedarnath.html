Kedarnath Temple

Kedarnath, Rudraprayag District, Uttarakhand

Home About History Geography Worship

Kedarnath Temple is one of the most sacred Hindu shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva. Located high in the Himalayas, it is traditionally associated with Panch Kedar, the twelve Jyotirlingas and the Uttarakhand Char Dham pilgrimage.

1. Which Deity Is Worshipped Here?

Lord Shiva is worshipped at Kedarnath in the form of a sacred stone lingam. The temple is one of the most important pilgrimage sites for devotees of Lord Shiva.

2. Who Visits and What Do They Pray For?

Pilgrims from across India and around the world visit Kedarnath to offer prayers to Lord Shiva and seek blessings, peace and spiritual strength.

3. History of Kedarnath

Kedarnath is an ancient pilgrimage destination associated with Hindu traditions and the legends of the Pandavas. The temple holds great religious and historical importance.

4. Geography and Location

Kedarnath Temple is situated in the Garhwal Himalayas of Rudraprayag district, near the Mandakini River and surrounded by magnificent mountain peaks.

5. Best Time to Visit

Kedarnath is generally visited during the temple's open season, usually from late spring to autumn. Visitors should check the official temple opening dates and weather conditions before planning their journey.

6. How to Reach Kedarnath

Visitors generally travel by road to Gaurikund. From there, the temple is reached by trekking, or by using permitted available transport services.
