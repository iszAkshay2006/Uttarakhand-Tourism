Chaukori

Discover the peaceful beauty of Chaukori, famous for its panoramic Himalayan views, tea gardens, fruit orchards and beautiful sunrises and sunsets.

Chaukori is a peaceful hill destination in the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand. Located at around 2,010 metres, it offers beautiful views of Himalayan peaks, green valleys, tea gardens and forests. It is an ideal destination for nature lovers and travellers seeking a quiet mountain experience.

Famous Viewpoints & Places

  • Panoramic views of Panchachuli peaks
  • Himalayan sunrise and sunset viewpoints
  • Chaukori tea gardens
  • Fruit orchards and nearby villages
  • Dharamgarh and Berinag

Things to Do

  • Enjoy nature walks through tea gardens.
  • Capture Himalayan mountain views.
  • Explore nearby villages and local culture.
  • Enjoy bird watching and photography.
  • Plan nearby trekking experiences.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November are generally good months to visit Chaukori. These periods offer pleasant weather and opportunities for clear mountain views.

Monsoon rain can affect mountain roads and outdoor activities. Check local weather and road conditions before travelling.

Culture & Local Life

Chaukori reflects the traditional culture of the Kumaon region. Visitors can explore nearby villages, local handicrafts, traditional food and the peaceful lifestyle of the Himalayan communities.

History & Local Significance

Chaukori is known for its cultural heritage and beautiful natural surroundings. The region has historical associations with the Chand dynasty and has attracted visitors, artists and notable personalities over time. Its tea gardens and traditional Kumaoni culture add to its distinctive identity.

Nearby Attractions

  • Patal Bhuvaneshwar: A famous limestone cave shrine located around 40 km from Chaukori.
  • Dharamgarh: A peaceful place known for spectacular Himalayan views.
  • Berinag: A small hill settlement known for its tea gardens and scenic surroundings.
  • Askot Wildlife Sanctuary: A protected natural area known for musk deer and Himalayan biodiversity.

Population & Settlement

Chaukori is a small hill settlement in Pithoragarh district. Its surroundings include villages, tea gardens, orchards and forested hills.

How to Reach Chaukori

By Air

The nearest major airport is Pantnagar Airport. Taxis and other road transport can be used for the onward journey.

By Train

Kathgodam is a major railway station used by travellers heading towards the Kumaon region. From there, Chaukori can be reached by road.

By Road

Chaukori is connected by motorable roads to Pithoragarh and other major towns of Kumaon. Taxis and local transport are available on commonly travelled routes.

Visitor Tips

  • Carry warm clothes, especially during winter.
  • Start early for sunrise and mountain viewpoints.
  • Check road and weather conditions before travelling.
  • Book accommodation in advance during busy seasons.
  • Carry reusable water bottles and avoid littering.
  • Respect local customs, wildlife and private property.

Responsible Travel

Help keep Chaukori clean by avoiding plastic waste. Support local homestays and small businesses, ask permission before photographing residents, and avoid disturbing wildlife or fragile forest areas.
