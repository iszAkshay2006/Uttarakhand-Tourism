UTTARAKHAND • DEV BHOOMI

Local Food Details

Discover the traditional flavors of Uttarakhand. From hearty mountain meals to delicious local sweets, every dish reflects the culture and traditions of the region.

REGIONAL FLAVORS

Simple Ingredients, Rich Traditions

Uttarakhand cuisine uses local grains, lentils, seasonal vegetables and aromatic spices. Garhwali and Kumaoni food traditions each offer distinctive flavors and recipes.

WHAT TO TRY

Traditional Uttarakhand Dishes

Local Cuisine

Kafuli

A traditional green-leaf dish made with leafy vegetables and spices, often served with rice.

Garhwali Food

Chainsoo

A hearty Garhwali dish prepared with roasted black gram dal and aromatic spices.

Traditional Snack

Urad Dal Pakode

Crispy lentil fritters enjoyed as a snack, especially with a hot cup of tea.

Kumaoni Sweet

Bal Mithai

A famous Kumaoni sweet made from roasted khoya and coated with small sugar balls.

Traditional Dessert

Jhangora Kheer

A creamy dessert prepared with barnyard millet, milk and sweetener.

Mountain Cuisine

Phaanu

A nutritious lentil-based dish traditionally prepared using soaked lentils.

Eat Local, Travel Thoughtfully

Ask about ingredients and spice levels before ordering. Try local eateries to discover regional recipes and support the communities you visit.
