Tehri Lake

New Tehri, Tehri Garhwal district, Uttarakhand

Tehri Lake is a beautiful artificial lake in Uttarakhand, formed by the Tehri Dam on the Bhagirathi River. Surrounded by mountains, it is a popular destination for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.

1. About Tehri Lake

Tehri Lake is one of the largest artificial lakes in India. It was created as part of the Tehri Hydroelectric Project. Its peaceful blue waters and surrounding Himalayan landscapes make it a famous tourist attraction.

2. Tehri Dam

Tehri Dam is a major engineering project built on the Bhagirathi River. The dam supports hydroelectric power generation, irrigation and water supply.

3. Adventure Activities

Visitors can enjoy activities such as boating, jet skiing, kayaking and other water sports, depending on availability, weather and local operating conditions.

4. Natural Beauty

The lake is surrounded by green hills and beautiful mountain scenery. It is a wonderful place to enjoy peaceful views, photography and relaxing time near the water.

5. Best Time to Visit and Travel Tips

The pleasant months from March to June and September to November are generally suitable for visiting. Check weather conditions and local activity timings before planning water sports. Follow safety instructions and use authorised activity operators.
