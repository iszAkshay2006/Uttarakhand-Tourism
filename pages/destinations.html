PLAN SMARTER

Travel Information

Prepare before you leave for the mountains.

🗓️ Choosing Your Season

Weather varies by destination and altitude. Monsoon can bring landslides and road closures. High-altitude areas can experience snow and cold.

🚗 Getting Around

Plan for winding mountain roads and longer travel times. Check transport and road conditions before departure.

🎒 What to Pack

Pack suitable footwear, layered clothing, rain protection, drinking water, personal medicines and a charged phone.

🛡️ Safety & Permits

Check official entry rules, registration requirements, weather alerts and trail access. Use authorised guides for adventure activities.

Important travel note

Opening dates, road access, permits and activity availability can change. Verify current information with official tourism, forest, temple and district authorities before travelling.
