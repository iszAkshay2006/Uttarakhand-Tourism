About Dhanaulti

Dhanaulti is a peaceful hill station in the Tehri Garhwal district of Uttarakhand. Located at an altitude of around 2,250 metres, it is surrounded by beautiful forests of deodar, oak and rhododendron trees.

Away from the busy tourist crowds, Dhanaulti offers a relaxing atmosphere, pleasant weather and wonderful views of the Himalayan mountains. It is a perfect destination for nature lovers, families and travellers looking for a peaceful getaway.

Popular Places to Visit

Dhanaulti Eco Park

Dhanaulti has two beautiful eco parks named Amber and Dhara. Surrounded by tall deodar and oak trees, these parks are perfect for nature walks, relaxation and spending time with family.

Surkanda Devi Temple

Located near Dhanaulti, Surkanda Devi Temple is an important religious destination and one of the 51 Shakti Peethas. Visitors can reach the temple through a scenic trek from Kaddukhal.

Deogarh Fort

Deogarh Fort is a historical attraction known for its ancient architecture, beautiful murals and nearby Jain temples. It offers a glimpse into the region's history.

Aaloo Khet

Aaloo Khet is a potato farm and a popular viewpoint near Dhanaulti. It is a peaceful place to enjoy open landscapes and capture beautiful photographs.

Things to Do in Dhanaulti

Trekking and Nature Walks

Explore the beautiful forest trails and enjoy peaceful walks through the deodar and oak forests. The trek to Surkanda Devi Temple is a popular experience.

Camping

Enjoy camping in the natural surroundings of Dhanaulti. The forests, open landscapes and mountain views make it a wonderful destination for outdoor stays.

Mountain Biking

Explore the scenic mountain roads and forest pathways through cycling and mountain biking.

Photography

Capture beautiful Himalayan views, green forests, peaceful landscapes and the natural beauty of Dhanaulti.

Best Time to Visit

April to October is considered a good time to visit Dhanaulti for pleasant weather and outdoor activities. During winter, the region may receive snowfall and becomes especially attractive for visitors who enjoy cold weather.

How to Reach Dhanaulti

By Air: The nearest airport is Jolly Grant Airport near Dehradun.

By Train: Dehradun and Rishikesh are the nearest major railway stations.

By Road: Dhanaulti is connected by road with Dehradun, Mussoorie and other major destinations of Uttarakhand.
