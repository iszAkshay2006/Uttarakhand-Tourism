Spiritual Destination

Kainchi Dham

Discover the peaceful spiritual sanctuary of Neem Karoli Baba, surrounded by the beautiful Kumaon hills of Uttarakhand.

About Kainchi Dham

Kainchi Dham is a famous spiritual destination located in the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand, near Nainital. It is renowned for the ashram associated with the revered saint Neem Karoli Baba, also known as Maharaj Ji.

Surrounded by green hills and situated beside the Shipra River, the ashram offers a peaceful atmosphere for prayer, meditation and spiritual reflection.

Neem Karoli Baba Ashram

The ashram is dedicated to Neem Karoli Baba and attracts devotees from India and around the world. Visitors come here for darshan, prayer and meditation.

Hanuman Temple

The main temple is dedicated to Lord Hanuman. Devotees visit the temple to offer prayers and experience its peaceful spiritual atmosphere.

Shipra River

The ashram is located beside the Shipra River. The surrounding riverbank and green hills add to the natural beauty of the place.

Annual Bhandara

The annual Bhandara is held on 15 June to mark the temple's Pratishtha Diwas. Devotees gather for prayers and community meals on this special occasion.

History & Spiritual Significance

Kainchi Dham is closely associated with Neem Karoli Baba. The first temple was inaugurated in June 1964. The ashram gradually developed into an important pilgrimage centre.

The name Kainchi is associated with two sharp hairpin bends on the nearby mountain road. The place is known for devotion, meditation and worship.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November are generally pleasant periods to visit Kainchi Dham. The annual Bhandara on 15 June is an especially important occasion for devotees.

The area can be crowded during festivals and holidays. Weather, road access and temple arrangements may change, so check official updates before travelling.

Visitor Guidelines

  • Respect the religious customs and peaceful atmosphere of the ashram.
  • Follow instructions given by temple authorities and volunteers.
  • Keep the temple premises and river surroundings clean.
  • Dress respectfully while visiting the temple.
  • Check photography rules before taking pictures inside the premises.
  • Be prepared for crowds during the annual Bhandara and busy seasons.

How to Reach Kainchi Dham

By Air

Pantnagar Airport is the nearest airport. From there, visitors can continue by taxi or road transport.

By Train

Kathgodam Railway Station is the nearest major railway station. Taxis and other road transport options are available from there.

By Road

Kainchi Dham is situated on the Nainital–Almora road, around 9 km from Bhowali and 17 km from Nainital.

Nearby Places

Nainital, Bhowali, Bhimtal and Mukteshwar are among the nearby destinations that visitors can explore.

Responsible Travel

Kainchi Dham is an important place of worship. Visitors should respect devotees, follow temple rules, avoid littering and help maintain the peaceful environment of the ashram.
