Char Dham Yatra • Uttarakhand, India

Gangotri Temple

Gangotri, Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand

Gangotri Temple is one of the four important pilgrimage destinations in Uttarakhand's Char Dham circuit.

1. Which Deity Is Worshipped Here?

Goddess Ganga is worshipped at Gangotri Temple.

2. Why Was the Temple Established? The Sacred Story

Hindu tradition tells that King Bhagiratha performed intense penance to bring the heavenly Ganga to Earth so that his ancestors could be liberated. Lord Shiva caught the powerful river in his matted hair and released it gently onto Earth. The Gangotri Temple was built as a place to worship Goddess Ganga near the river's sacred Himalayan source region. The main glacier source, Gaumukh, lies farther upstream.

3. Why Do Pilgrims Visit?

The temple honours Goddess Ganga and the river's role in purification, devotion and ancestral rites. Pilgrims visit to offer prayers and seek spiritual blessings.

4. Geographical Setting

Gangotri lies beside the Bhagirathi River in the Garhwal Himalaya. Gaumukh, the glacier terminus traditionally associated with the river's source, is farther upstream and reached by a separate trek.

5. Worship Practices and Travel Advice

Common observances include darshan, aarti, prayers and offerings to Goddess Ganga. Pilgrims should check seasonal opening dates, permits and trail conditions before travelling upstream.

Further information: Official reference website. The sacred stories are described as Hindu religious traditions, not as independently verified historical events.
