Katarmal Sun Temple – Almora district, Uttarakhand.

Famous Viewpoints & Places

  • Main Sun Temple
  • Subsidiary shrines
  • Stone-carved façades
  • Surrounding Kumaon valley viewpoints

Things to Do

Explore heritage architecture, learn about religious history, photograph the stone carvings and enjoy a peaceful visit.

Best Time to Visit

March–June and September–November. Mornings offer softer light for viewing the carvings.

Weather, road access and opening dates can change. Check local advisories shortly before travelling.

Culture & Local Life

Kumaoni heritage and Hindu sun worship. Local temple customs and traditions remain important.

History & Local Significance

The temple is generally dated to the medieval period and is associated with worship of Surya, the Hindu Sun deity. Its stone architecture is a major example of Kumaon's temple heritage.

Architecture & Temple Features

The temple complex is known for its main shrine, subsidiary shrines and detailed stone construction. Visitors should observe the carvings carefully without touching or damaging fragile surfaces.

Population & Settlement

The temple is a heritage site, not a populated town; nearby villages have small populations.

Visitor Tips

Allow around 1–2 hours for a visit. Respect the monument, avoid touching fragile carvings and check access arrangements.

  • Carry drinking water and take all litter back with you.
  • Respect local customs and worship areas.
  • Do not climb on or damage historic structures.
  • Ask permission before photographing local people.
  • Check local road and weather conditions before travelling.

Responsible Travel

Choose locally run services where possible, support local communities and avoid disturbing the historic monument or its surroundings. Help preserve this important part of Kumaon's cultural heritage.
