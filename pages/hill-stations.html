PLAN BEFORE YOU GO

Mountain Travel Tips

  • Check weather forecasts and road conditions before departure.
  • Carry warm layers, comfortable footwear and rain protection when needed.
  • Allow extra travel time on winding mountain roads.
  • Keep natural areas clean and follow local safety instructions.
