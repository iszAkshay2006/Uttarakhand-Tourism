Char Dham Yatra • Uttarakhand, India

Yamunotri Temple

Yamunotri, Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand

Yamunotri Temple is one of the four important pilgrimage destinations in Uttarakhand's Char Dham circuit.

1. Which Deity Is Worshipped Here?

Goddess Yamuna is worshipped here. The shrine honours the sacred river Yamuna, whose source region lies in the high Himalayas.

2. Why Was the Temple Established? The Sacred Story

In Hindu tradition, Yamuna is the daughter of Surya, the Sun God, and the sister of Yama, the god associated with death. Devotees believe that worshipping Yamuna can bring spiritual protection and help free them from fear. The temple was developed as a place of worship near the sacred river source region; the modern shrine has been repaired and rebuilt over time because of the harsh mountain climate.

3. Why Do Pilgrims Visit?

The temple exists to honour Goddess Yamuna and the sacred river. Pilgrims visit to offer prayers, seek blessings for well-being and perform rituals connected with the river.

4. Geographical Setting

The temple stands in a narrow Himalayan valley near hot springs and the upper Yamuna catchment. The source glacier is higher in the mountains and is not the same as the temple location.

5. Worship Practices and Travel Advice

Devotees offer prayers to Goddess Yamuna and may cook rice or potatoes in the hot spring water as a traditional offering, where local rules permit. The route is seasonal and weather-sensitive.

Further information: Official reference website. The sacred stories are described as Hindu religious traditions, not as independently verified historical events.
