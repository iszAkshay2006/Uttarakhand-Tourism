Panch Kedar • Uttarakhand, India

Madhyamaheshwar Temple

Mansoona region, Rudraprayag district, Uttarakhand

Madhyamaheshwar Temple is one of the five sacred Shiva shrines traditionally known as Panch Kedar. This page covers the deity, worship, beliefs, history and geographical setting.

1. Which Deity Is Worshipped Here?

Lord Shiva is worshipped at Madhyamaheshwar. In Panch Kedar tradition, Shiva's middle torso or navel is believed to have appeared here.

2. Who Visits and What Do They Pray For?

Pilgrims worship Shiva and pray for peace, family well-being, protection and spiritual merit. The temple is an important stop in the Panch Kedar pilgrimage.

3. Religious Beliefs, Vows and Legends

According to the Panch Kedar legend, the Pandavas searched for Shiva after the Mahabharata war, and the deity's body appeared at five places. The middle part is associated with Madhyamaheshwar; this is a sacred mythological account.

4. History and Sacred Traditions

Madhyamaheshwar is a traditional Panch Kedar shrine in the Garhwal Himalaya. Nearby Budha Madhyamaheshwar is known for its rocky shrine and panoramic mountain views. The site combines seasonal pilgrimage with a long-standing local religious tradition.

5. Temple Area and Dimensions

A verified official total area for the temple and all surrounding sacred grounds is not consistently published. The main temple is a compact stone structure; an exact footprint should be taken from a reliable architectural survey.

6. Geography and Access

The temple stands at about 3,289 metres in a green Himalayan valley, with views toward the Chaukhamba and other peaks. The trek is long and steep; published route distances vary by starting point and route updates, so check current local guidance before travel.

7. Worship Practices and Visitor Advice

Pilgrims commonly perform darshan, prayers and offerings under local temple customs. The shrine is seasonal due to snow; verify opening dates, accommodation and route conditions before starting the trek.
