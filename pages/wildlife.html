Discover Uttarakhand's Wildlife

Explore the forests, national parks and diverse wildlife of Uttarakhand. Discover the natural beauty of the Himalayas and learn how to experience it responsibly.

🐅

Royal Bengal Tiger

Uttarakhand's forests provide habitat for the Bengal tiger. Jim Corbett National Park is a well-known destination for wildlife tourism.

🐘

Asian Elephant

Elephants inhabit forest and grassland ecosystems in parts of Uttarakhand. Observe wildlife only from a safe and responsible distance.

🐆

Snow Leopard

This elusive mountain cat inhabits high-altitude Himalayan regions. Sightings are rare, and its habitat deserves special protection.

🦌

Himalayan Musk Deer

The Himalayan musk deer lives in mountain forests and alpine habitats. Conservation helps protect this threatened wildlife species.

Wildlife Destinations

Jim Corbett National Park

Known for its rich biodiversity, forest landscapes and tiger conservation.

Rajaji National Park

Explore the Shivalik landscape, forest ecosystems and elephant habitat.

Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve

A Himalayan conservation landscape known for its unique mountain ecosystems.

Travel Responsibly

Follow park rules, keep a safe distance from animals, avoid feeding wildlife, do not litter and use authorised guides and safari services where required.
