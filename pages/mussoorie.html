Mussoorie

The Queen of Hills – A beautiful Himalayan hill station

Mussoorie is a famous hill station in Dehradun district of Uttarakhand. Known as the Queen of Hills, it is popular for its beautiful mountain views, pleasant weather, waterfalls and scenic valleys.

1. About Mussoorie

Mussoorie is one of the most popular tourist destinations in Uttarakhand. Surrounded by green hills and Himalayan views, it attracts visitors throughout the year.

2. History of Mussoorie

Mussoorie developed as a hill station during British rule in India. Its pleasant climate and beautiful surroundings made it a popular summer retreat.

3. Geography and Location

Mussoorie is situated in the Garhwal Himalayan region of Uttarakhand, at an elevation of around 2,000 metres. It offers beautiful views of the Doon Valley and surrounding mountains.

4. Things to Do in Mussoorie

Visitors can explore Mall Road, enjoy the ropeway to Gun Hill, visit Kempty Falls, take nature walks and enjoy the mountain scenery.

5. Best Time to Visit

March to June is popular for pleasant weather and sightseeing. September to November is also a good time to enjoy clear mountain views and peaceful surroundings.

6. How to Reach Mussoorie

The nearest railway station is Dehradun Railway Station. Mussoorie is connected to Dehradun by road, and taxis and buses are available for the journey.
