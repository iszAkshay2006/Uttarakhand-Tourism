THE HIMALAYAN PILGRIMAGE

Discover the Char Dham of Uttarakhand.

The traditional Char Dham Yatra of Uttarakhand connects four important pilgrimage destinations: Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath.

These journeys involve mountain roads, changing weather and, in several cases, demanding walks. Check current opening dates, registration requirements and official advisories before departure.
