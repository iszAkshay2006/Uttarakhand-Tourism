PLAN WITH CARE

Before You Begin Your Pilgrimage

Mountain journeys are more enjoyable when you plan ahead. Keep these essentials in mind.

01

Check Temple Dates

Opening periods and darshan arrangements can change. Check official announcements before travelling.

02

Prepare for the Weather

Carry suitable layers, comfortable footwear and rain protection for mountain conditions.

03

Respect Local Traditions

Follow temple rules, dress respectfully and keep sacred places clean.
