Almora

Discover the beauty, culture and peaceful hills of Almora. Experience the charm of traditional Kumaoni life and beautiful Himalayan mountain views.

Welcome to Almora

Almora is a beautiful hill town in the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand. Surrounded by green hills and scenic mountain views, it is known for its natural beauty, rich culture, traditional markets and peaceful atmosphere. Almora is a wonderful destination for travellers who want to experience the charm of the Himalayas.

About Almora

Almora is famous for its traditional culture, beautiful architecture, local handicrafts and panoramic views of the Himalayan mountain ranges.

Famous Attractions

Popular places around Almora include Kasar Devi Temple, Bright End Corner, Zero Point at Binsar and Chitai Golu Devta Temple.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November are generally pleasant periods to explore Almora and its surrounding areas.

Things to Do

Enjoy Himalayan views, explore local markets, visit temples, try Kumaoni food and experience the peaceful mountain lifestyle.

Local Food

Try traditional Kumaoni dishes such as Bhatt ki Churkani, Aloo ke Gutke, Bal Mithai and Singori.

How to Reach

Almora can be reached by road from nearby cities. Kathgodam is a major nearby railway station, while Pantnagar is the nearest airport.
