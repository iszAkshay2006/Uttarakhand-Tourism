Traditional Flavours

A Taste of Devbhoomi

Uttarakhand's cuisine reflects its mountain lifestyle, local agriculture, and rich cultural heritage. Pulses, leafy greens, grains, and aromatic spices are important ingredients in many traditional dishes.

Discover the Cuisine

Popular Local Dishes

Explore some much-loved foods from Uttarakhand.

Kafuli

Traditional Main Course

A popular Pahadi dish prepared with leafy greens, spices, and a thick, flavourful gravy.

Chainsoo

Pahadi Speciality

A hearty Garhwali dish made from roasted and ground black gram, cooked with spices.

Urad Dal Pakode

Mountain Snack

Crispy lentil fritters enjoyed with chutney, especially alongside a cup of hot tea.

Bal Mithai

Famous Sweet

A well-known Kumaoni sweet made from roasted khoya and coated with small sugar balls.

Jhangora Kheer

Traditional Dessert

A creamy dessert prepared using jhangora, also known as barnyard millet, and milk.

Phaanu

Garhwali Cuisine

A nourishing lentil-based preparation, traditionally cooked slowly for a rich taste.
