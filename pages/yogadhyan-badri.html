Panch Badri • Uttarakhand, India

Yogadhyan Badri Temple

Pandukeshwar, Chamoli district, Uttarakhand

Yogadhyan Badri Temple is one of the sacred shrines traditionally included in the Panch Badri pilgrimage circuit. This guide covers the deity, worship practices, local beliefs, history and geographical setting.

1. Which Deity Is Worshipped Here?

The temple is dedicated to Lord Vishnu as Yogadhyan Badri. The sacred image is traditionally described as being in a meditative posture.

2. Who Comes Here to Worship?

Devotees worship Lord Vishnu and pray for peace, concentration, family well-being and spiritual progress. The name and iconography emphasize meditation and devotion.

3. Religious Beliefs and Vows

Local tradition associates Pandukeshwar with King Pandu, father of the Pandavas, and says he worshipped or meditated here. Devotees may make personal vows and offer prayers, with results understood as matters of faith.

4. History and Sacred Traditions

Yogadhyan Badri is one of the Panch Badri shrines. Pandukeshwar is also associated with old religious traditions and nearby sacred sites. The account connecting King Pandu to the place belongs to Hindu tradition; it should not be presented as independently proven historical fact.

5. Temple Area and Dimensions

The temple building and its immediate precinct are not generally described through one consistent official area figure in square metres. This page therefore gives no invented measurement; consult a temple or heritage survey for a verified dimension.

6. Geographical Setting

Pandukeshwar lies on the route between Joshimath and Badrinath in the upper Alaknanda valley. Its mountain setting, altitude and seasonal weather influence travel and pilgrimage.

7. Worship Practices and Visiting Advice

Common observances include darshan, prayer and offerings according to temple customs. Confirm current opening times and festival arrangements locally, especially when travelling during winter or bad weather.
