BE A RESPONSIBLE EXPLORER

Before You Set Up Camp

Mountain weather can change quickly. Plan for the conditions, keep a backup route and use local expertise where needed.

  • Confirm campsite permissions, opening dates and road access.
  • Carry warm layers, rain protection, a first-aid kit and enough water.
  • Use established campsites; avoid fragile meadows and protected habitats.
  • Pack out all rubbish and never leave a fire unattended.
  • Check weather alerts and follow instructions from local authorities.
