KNOW THE STATE

Uttarakhand at a Glance

Uttarakhand lies in the Indian Himalayas and is known for mountain landscapes, rivers, forests, pilgrimage routes and diverse Garhwali and Kumaoni heritage. Dehradun is its winter capital.
